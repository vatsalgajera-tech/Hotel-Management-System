ABOUT US

One of Dubai’s most iconic hotels from the past is the Chicago Beach Hotel, which stood on the site where the Madinat Jumeirah now exists. Opened in the 1970s, this hotel was known for its beachfront luxury and was a favorite among travelers before being demolished in the late 1990s to make way for modern developments. The hotel was named after the Chicago Bridge & Iron Company, which had offshore storage tanks in the area.
