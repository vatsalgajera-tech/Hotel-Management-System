Luxurious Escape

Welcome to Celestial View, a luxurious escape in the heart of Dubai. Celestial View Hotels & Resorts is a luxury hospitality brand headquartered in Dubai, UAE, renowned for its world-class service. Established in 1997, It is often referred to as the world’s only 7-star hotel. Celestial View operates in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, London, Maldives, and other top destinations. With a focus on innovation and sustainability, Celestial View continually enhances guest experiences. Whether for romantic getaways, family vacations, or corporate stays, Celestial View delivers unmatched excellence.
